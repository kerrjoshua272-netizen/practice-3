About Me

Blending Bahamian heritage with functional spatial storytelling

I’m Kalila Roberts, a 16-year-old Bahamian girl that attends St. John’s College. I hope to pursue a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Interior Design. Growing up, I had very little exposure to art, but I was enlightened by the little bits I was exposed to.

The art I was exposed to was my uncle's art on the walls of my house and Junkanoo, which is a cultural and community-driven festival. It’s driven through rhythmic sounds, music, and dance with an eccentric display of colors built from repurposing discarded or reused items. I thrive on curiosity and creativity—I’m always deep in my “what if” and “I wonder why” thoughts.

My Ethereal Dream Bedroom

26/10/2026 • Residential Concept

Concept Statement

This bedroom is designed as an escape, a place where soft luxury meets calm beauty. Gentle, flowing drapery and bedding that feels like clouds wrap the space in comfort. Smooth metallic touches and warm light from fixtures add a touch of refinement. The color scheme stays soothing—plaster white, soft champagne, and subtle twilight tones work together to create a gentle backdrop. Handcrafted details bring a sense of creativity making the space feel personal and alive, shifting from a calming morning retreat to a cozy intimate nightscape.

AutoCAD Floor Plan Zones

  • Primary bedroom with TV stand and dresser
  • Double hanging closet, single hanging closet, and closet dresser
  • En-suite bath with tub and shower

Finishes, Fixtures & Furniture

  • Finishes: Floor tiles, wall paint in Supernova N510-4, and shower tiles
  • Fixtures: Ceiling fan, bathroom chandelier, bathtub & shelf, bathroom cupboard and sink, toilet room, built-in closet shelf, built-in shelf, bedside lamps, shower, bathroom mirror, and recessed lights
  • Furniture & Fabrics: Closet dresser, bed frame, vanity set, vanity chair, room dresser, lounge chair, bedding, closet rug, bedroom rug, and bathroom rug

Harbour Side Restaurant Concept

28/10/2026 • Hospitality Concept

Concept Statement

Rich historical lineage and vibrant cultural expression come together in a restaurant influenced by Junkanoo, Bahamian culture, and history. The design honors Lucayan heritage through hand-woven straw, smooth coral stone, and limestone, balances the Age of Piracy and early shipbuilding with coarse rope, tarnished/dull brass and metals, and weathered driftwood, and channels Junkanoo and Kalik energy with ocean blues, sunlight golds, carnival hues, bold geometric patterns, and oversized lighting inspired by Junkanoo banners and headpieces.

AutoCAD Floor Plan Zones

  • Entry, waiting area benches, host stand, and central feature piece
  • Main dining area, banquette seating, and bar tops
  • Bar, back of house, kitchen, windows, plants, and deck

Feature Piece, Finishes & Furniture

  • Focal Element: Junkanoo-inspired feature piece
  • Finishes & Fixtures: Lighting fixture, interior/exterior paint including Bluebird Skies, bar counter, bar backsplash, and floor tile color
  • Furniture & Fabrics: Dining chair, dining table, booth seating, high top table, bar/high top chairs, and Bahamian patterned fabrics for chair/booth cushions and upholstery
